# The Factorization Residual
## Dissolving Φ_PN, and the two computable residuals that survive

> Status: design paper, and the most self-critical of this corpus. This essay retracts a beautiful idea — the net-level scalar Φ_PN of [`principles.md`](principles.md) and [`latent-architecture.md`](latent-architecture.md) — and keeps only what is theorem-backed, monotone, integer or sign-definite, non-vacuous, and *measurable over the corpus*. The core theory is Michael's; the residual framing and the Rank-Theorem link proposed here are mine (Daniel Dyer, with Claude), and each load-bearing claim below names the falsifier or proof obligation that would sink it. The orientation this sits within is the [README's inversion](README.md). The implementation hooks live in [`BACKLOG.md`](../../BACKLOG.md) epic H (H2 composition operators, H2a Φ_bound, H2b Φ_inv).

**Abstract.** The capstone of the principles essay was a single net-level number, Φ_PN, "measuring how much a net refuses to be a product," advertised as the one quantity whose definition requires all four organizing principles at once, and quietly shadowing the Φ of Integrated Information Theory. Examined ruthlessly, two of its three claims are mirages. As a boolean — "does the property factor over this cut, yes or no" — it is assume-guarantee reasoning, a mature field with nothing left to name. As a net-level aggregate it sums incommensurable units: a place bound (tokens) added to an invariant-rank defect (dimensions) added to a liveness bit is a category error wearing the costume of a measurement. And the "needs all four roots, built to compute it" necessity is an aesthetic claim, not a mathematical one — Φ_PN is no more forced by the architecture than any other functional one could write over the same parts. This essay dissolves all three, without regret, because the honesty is the point. What survives is narrower and real: **two per-property factorization residuals**, each defined over a single property where the composition rule is genuine, each provably zero on a characterized class, each strictly positive on an exhibited witness, each carrying its minimizing cut as a checkable object. They are **Φ_bound** (a schedule-first boundedness residual) and **Φ_inv** (an invariant rank-defect residual whose link to the Rank Theorem is the genuinely new piece). The deliverable is not the metaphysics. It is the *distribution of these residuals over the MCC NUPN corpus, indexed by net class* — a measurement no one has reported.

---

## 1. What is dissolved, and why the dissolution is the contribution

The capstone as written ([`principles.md`](principles.md) §Capstone; [`latent-architecture.md`](latent-architecture.md) §7) made three claims. Take them one at a time, each with the reason it fails.

**Claim 1 — Φ as a boolean is a new object.** "Φ_PN > 0 means the system is irreducible; no decomposition recovers it." Read at the level of a single property and a single cut, this boolean is: *does the conjunction of the parts' verdicts, recombined under the property's composition rule, equal the whole's verdict?* That is exactly the question assume-guarantee and compositional verification have asked since the 1980s — Misra–Chandy, Pnueli, Abadi–Lamper; for open Petri nets specifically, the modular and compositional school (open nets, Baldan–Corradini–Ehrig–Heckel; Kronecker descriptors, Buchholz; Donatelli). "Does P factor over this partition" is a solved *question shape*. Re-badging it Φ adds a Greek letter, not a result. *Falsifier of the original claim:* point to any compositional-verification paper that asks whether a property of the whole follows from properties of the parts across an interface. There are hundreds. The boolean is dissolved.

**Claim 2 — Φ as a net-level scalar is a measurement.** The formula in [`latent-architecture.md`](latent-architecture.md) §7 minimizes, over cuts, a distance δ between the whole's verdict and a tensor of sub-verdicts, where δ is "verdict-join for booleans, direct-sum-plus-interface-correction for invariant spaces, max for place bounds." The phrase *for booleans … for invariant spaces … for place bounds* is the tell. There is no single δ. Each property has its own unit: liveness is a bit, a place bound is a token count in ℕ ∪ {∞}, an invariant defect is a dimension count in ℕ. A *net-level* Φ that ranges over all properties at once must add a bit to a token to a dimension. That sum has no meaning — it is not invariant under any rescaling of the parts, it has no zero that means the same thing across summands, and its ordering is an artifact of the arbitrary weights one picks to make the addition typecheck. A scalar that changes when you relabel your units is not measuring the net; it is measuring your labeling. *Falsifier of the original claim:* exhibit two properties whose residuals are in commensurable units and a principled rule for adding them. None exists. The net-level scalar is dissolved.

**Claim 3 — Φ needs all four principles, and the architecture was built to compute it.** This is the load-bearing rhetorical move of the principles essay, and it is the weakest. "Φ_PN requires all five prior structures at once" is true of *any* functional rich enough to mention a verdict, a decomposition, a completion, and a witness — which is to say it is true of the project's whole surface, and so distinguishes nothing. The architecture was built to *decide properties with checkable certificates*; that it *can also* be read as computing a Φ is a fact about the expressiveness of the parts, not a teleology. The necessity is aesthetic: it feels inevitable because the essay arranged the four principles to converge on it, the way a mystery arranges its clues to converge on the detective's chosen suspect. *Falsifier:* produce a second, equally "inevitable" capstone functional over the same four principles — boundedness-defect, or invariant-defect, or any of a dozen others. The ease of producing it (this essay produces two) is the disproof of uniqueness. The necessity claim is dissolved.

What is left after these three subtractions is not nothing. It is the *schema* — minimize, over a decomposition lattice, a factorization residual for *one* property whose composition rule is genuine — instantiated only where the residual is well-typed, sign-definite, and theorem-anchored. The schema is sound; the scalar was the overreach. Keeping the schema and discarding the scalar is the entire move of this essay.

## 2. The schema, stated honestly

Fix a single property $P$ for which there is a *genuine* composition rule $\otimes_P$ — meaning: a way to combine the in-block answers that is correct in at least one direction and whose failure is the thing we want to measure. Fix a net $N$ and a decomposition lattice $\Pi(N)$ — the cuts the structure permits. For a cut $\pi \in \Pi(N)$ with blocks $\{B\}$, define a per-property residual

$$\Phi_P(N) \;=\; \min_{\pi \in \Pi(N)} \; \sum_{x} \Big( \, q_P^{\otimes}(\pi)_x \;-\; q_P(N)_x \, \Big),$$

where $q_P(N)$ is the true global quantity (a per-place bound, or the dimension of a kernel), $q_P^{\otimes}(\pi)$ is what the blocks compute *in isolation*, recombined by $\otimes_P$ under an explicit **interface correction**, the index $x$ ranges over the coordinates in which the two are comparable, and the summands are *all in one unit* because $P$ is fixed. The minimizing cut $\pi^\*$ is the **witness** — a checkable object, in the sense of [`the-checkable-frontier.md`](the-checkable-frontier.md): given $\pi^\*$ one recomputes both sides and reads off the residual without trusting the procedure that found it.

Three discipline rules make an instance of this schema worth building, and an instance that fails any of them is not worth building:

1. **Well-signed.** The composition must be a sound over- (or under-) approximation in a *fixed* direction, so the residual has one sign. If $q^{\otimes} \geq q$ always, the residual is $\geq 0$ and means "the parts overestimate the whole by this much." A residual that can flip sign measures nothing. *Obligation:* a monotonicity proof.
2. **Theorem-backed zero.** There must be a characterized class on which $\Phi_P = 0$ provably, by an existing theorem — not by absence of a counterexample in the test suite. A residual whose zero-set is unknown is a heuristic, not a measurement. *Obligation:* name the theorem.
3. **Non-vacuous.** There must be an exhibited net on which $\Phi_P > 0$ (or $= \infty$). A residual that is always zero is a tautology dressed as a discovery. *Obligation:* construct the positive witness.

Exactly two properties pass all three today: boundedness and invariant rank. They are Φ_bound and Φ_inv. Liveness does **not** pass — its composition rule is the boolean of Claim 1, assume-guarantee, with no quantitative residual to measure — and so liveness is correctly *excluded*, which is the discipline working as designed.

## 3. Φ_bound — the boundedness-factorization residual (schedule first)

### 3.1 Definition

The cut $\pi$ partitions the places of $N$ into blocks. The natural source of blocks is the NUPN unit cover already parsed into a forest in [`api/pnml/nupn.rs`](../../petrivet/src/api/pnml/nupn.rs) (each `<unit>` owns local places; cutting the tree at any antichain of units yields a partition), with the S-component cover of [`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs) (Hack's S-components, the strongly-connected S-subnets whose token sums bound their places) as the fallback when no NUPN block is present. For a block $B$, let $N^{\otimes}\!\restriction\! B$ be the **opened** sub-net: $B$'s places and transitions, with every transition that crosses the interface turned into a *free source/sink* — it may produce or consume tokens at the cut boundary without constraint. Let $b^{\otimes}(p)$ be the structural place bound computed *inside* $B$ on this opened sub-net (the S-system per-place bound `M₀(S′)` of [`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs):288, or the T-net minimum-circuit-token bound, both already characterized there), and let $b(N)_p$ be the true global structural bound. Then

$$\Phi_{\mathrm{bound}}(N) \;=\; \min_{\pi}\; \sum_{p} \big( b^{\otimes}(p) - b(N)_p \big) \;\in\; \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}.$$

Opening a block can only *loosen* its bounds — a free interface transition is the most permissive boundary condition — so $b^{\otimes} \geq b$ place-by-place, and each summand is $\geq 0$. This is discipline rule 1, and it is the monotonicity obligation:

> **Monotonicity (Φ_bound ≥ 0).** *Proof obligation (BACKLOG H2a).* For every block $B$ and place $p \in B$, the in-block opened bound $b^{\otimes}(p)$ dominates the global bound $b(N)_p$, because every reachable marking of $N$ projects to a marking of the opened $B$ (the interface transitions, freed, can reproduce the projected token flow), so the in-block reachable set contains the projected global reachable set, so its supremum is no smaller. *Falsifier:* a net and cut where opening a block tightens some place's bound — which would mean a freed interface transition removes reachable markings, contradicting "free." The proof is the falsifier's impossibility.

### 3.2 The theorem-backed zero

Φ_bound is **provably zero** on two characterized classes, by theorems already transcribed in [`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs):

- **Live bounded free-choice nets covered by S-components (Hack's boundedness theorem).** [`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs):279–291: a live free-choice system is bounded iff every place belongs to an S-component, and then `max{M(s)} = min{M₀(S′) : S′ an S-component containing s}`. Take the cut to be the S-component cover. Each place's global bound *is* its in-component token sum; opening the components changes nothing, because the bound was always read off a single component. So $b^{\otimes}(p) = b(N)_p$ for the minimizing cut, and $\Phi_{\mathrm{bound}} = 0$.
- **Strongly connected T-nets (the circuit-token theorem).** [`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs):181–188: a place $s$ of a live T-system is $k$-bounded iff it lies on a circuit, with `max{M(s)} = min{M₀(γ) : γ ∋ s}`. The circuits are the blocks; the bound is a within-circuit invariant; opening preserves it. $\Phi_{\mathrm{bound}} = 0$.

This is discipline rule 2 discharged twice. The zero-set is not "nets we happened to test"; it is *exactly the classes where boundedness is a within-block conservation law*, which is the precise content of "the property factors over the decomposition."

### 3.3 Non-vacuity: where it is positive

Φ_bound is strictly positive — indeed $\infty$ — on nets bounded *only* through cross-block synchronization. The canonical witness: two blocks each containing an unbounded internal counter, where boundedness holds globally because a shared interface place caps the two counters against each other (a producer/consumer with a finite shared buffer, cut between producer and consumer). In isolation each opened block has an unbounded place ($b^{\otimes} = \infty$); globally the place is bounded ($b(N) < \infty$); the residual is $\infty$ at every cut that separates them. This is discipline rule 3: an exhibited net, constructible as a fixture, on which the residual is decisively non-zero. *Falsifier of non-vacuity:* if every constructible net had $\Phi_{\mathrm{bound}} = 0$, the residual would be a tautology — but the producer/consumer-with-shared-buffer witness exists, so it is not.

### 3.4 The deliverable

The point is not the definition. It is the **distribution of $\Phi_{\mathrm{bound}}$ over a sample of the MCC NUPN corpus, indexed by `NetClass`** ([`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs)). The NUPN unit forest gives every corpus model a natural cut for free ([`api/pnml/nupn.rs`](../../petrivet/src/api/pnml/nupn.rs)); the question is empirical and unreported: *across real concurrent-system models, how often is boundedness a within-unit fact, and how often does it live in the cross-unit synchronization?* A histogram — fraction at zero (factors cleanly), fraction finite-positive (factors with a measurable residual), fraction infinite (does not factor at all) — sliced by net class, is a measurement of how modular real Petri nets are with respect to boundedness. That table is the contribution of H2a; the residual is just the instrument that produces it. *Falsifier of the deliverable's interest:* if the histogram is degenerate — all zero, or all infinite — the measurement is uninteresting (though still honest). The bet is that it is not, and the bet is settled by running it, not by arguing it.

## 4. Φ_inv — the invariant rank-defect residual (the novel one)

### 4.1 Definition

This is the deeper and more novel object, because its composition rule is linear algebra rather than order, and its zero-defect link reaches a theorem the architecture has not yet built. The global conservation laws of a net are the P-invariants: the kernel of the transposed incidence matrix, $\ker(C^\top)$, where $C = \text{Post} - \text{Pre}$ ([`principles.md`](principles.md) §2.2; the exact-rational core that computes this kernel is BACKLOG B0). Its dimension counts the independent conservation laws of the whole net. Each block $B$ also has its *own* local invariants — the kernel of its restricted incidence matrix. The residual is the count of conservation laws the global net has that **no single block can see**:

$$\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}}(N) \;=\; \min_{\pi}\; \Big( \dim \ker(C^\top) \;-\; \dim\big( \textstyle\bigoplus_{B \in \pi} \mathrm{Inv}^{\circ}(B) \big) \Big) \;\in\; \mathbb{N},$$

where $\mathrm{Inv}^{\circ}(B)$ is the block-local invariant space *after the interface correction* — and the interface correction is the entire mathematical content, the place where (as the compositional school has long known) the difficulty lives. The correction is made explicit by **zeroing the interface-coupled coordinates**: a block-local invariant is admitted into the direct sum only on the place-coordinates internal to $B$, with the coordinates on interface places (those shared with another block) set to zero, so that the direct sum is over genuinely disjoint coordinate sets and no local law is double-counted or allowed to claim an interface place it does not control alone. With that correction the direct sum is a *subspace* of the global kernel (every interface-corrected local invariant is a global invariant), so the dimension difference is $\geq 0$.

> **Non-negativity and basis-freeness (Φ_inv ∈ ℕ, integer, basis-independent).** *Proof obligation (BACKLOG H2b).* After the interface correction, $\bigoplus_B \mathrm{Inv}^{\circ}(B) \subseteq \ker(C^\top)$, so the difference of dimensions is a non-negative integer; and because both terms are dimensions of vector spaces, the value is independent of any choice of basis for the invariants — it is a property of the net and the cut, not of how one writes the semiflows down. *Falsifier:* an interface-corrected local invariant that is *not* a global invariant (which would break the subspace inclusion and the sign), or a value that changes under a change of invariant basis (which would break basis-freeness). The correction is *defined* to make the first impossible; the second is automatic for dimensions. The remaining genuine risk is that the correction as stated is too aggressive (zeroing a coordinate that a local invariant legitimately needs, undercounting the local space) — that is the obligation to discharge with the worked S-component examples of [`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs), not a thing to assert.

### 4.2 The link to the Rank Theorem — the genuinely new claim

The novel piece, and the one I flag as **pending a full literature check against Kronecker and compositional methods**, is the connection of $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}}$ to the Rank Theorem. The free-choice simultaneous liveness-and-boundedness characterization at [`core/class.rs`](../../petrivet/src/core/class.rs):293–298 includes the condition `rank C = c − 1`, where $c$ is the number of clusters (the place-transition coupling components, computed by the union-find of BACKLOG B2). Rank and nullity are complementary: $\dim \ker(C^\top) = |P| - \text{rank}\, C$. On a net where the rank deficit is governed by the cluster structure, $\dim \ker(C^\top) = |P| - (c-1)$, and the *global* conservation-law count is pinned by the cluster count $c$ — a single quotient invariant. The conjecture Φ_inv makes precise is:

> **Conjecture (Φ_inv as a cluster-vs-cut defect).** *Open, BACKLOG H2b.* When the decomposition cut $\pi$ is aligned with the cluster quotient, the block-local invariant spaces account for exactly the within-cluster conservation laws, and $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}}$ counts precisely the conservation laws that arise from *cross-cluster* rank deficit — the laws the Rank Theorem attributes to the global $c$ but no single cluster realizes. In the free-choice case where `rank C = c − 1` holds globally, $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}}$ relates the *global* $c$ to the *sum of per-block cluster counts*, measuring how much the net's conservation structure is irreducibly global. *Falsifier:* a free-choice net where the cluster-aligned cut gives $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}}$ uncorrelated with $c$ minus the per-block cluster sum. The conjecture is checkable on every `class.rs` fixture the moment B0 (rank), B1 (invariants), and B2 (clusters) land; it is the cheapest possible disproof, and it must be run before the link is claimed in the thesis.

This is the one place this essay reaches past the established literature, and so it is the one place it is most cautious. The residual framing is mine; the Rank-Theorem link is a *conjecture with a named falsifier*, not a theorem, and the literature check (does the Kronecker-descriptor / compositional-invariant school already have this, under another name?) is a prerequisite to any claim of novelty, not a formality. *If the literature has it, the contribution shrinks to the measurement; if it does not, the link is the new mathematics.* Either outcome is reported honestly.

### 4.3 The deliverable, again

As with Φ_bound, the object to ship is the **distribution of $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}}$ over the corpus, indexed by net class** — how many of a real net's conservation laws are global facts no module sees. A net of independent modules has $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}} = 0$ (every conservation law is local); a tightly coupled net has $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}}$ large (the interesting invariants are all cross-cut). The histogram measures the *linear* modularity of the corpus, complementary to Φ_bound's *order* modularity. Both are measurements no one has reported, and both are produced by running the instrument, not by defending it.

## 5. Prior art: this is the compositional school's recombination, asking a different question

It would be dishonest to present these residuals as un-anchored novelty. The recombination $\otimes_P$ — *direct sum plus interface correction* — is precisely the move of the Kronecker-descriptor and compositional/modular school of Petri net analysis. **Open nets** (Baldan–Corradini–Ehrig–Heckel; Kindler) formalize nets with interface places and the gluing of parts along them. **Kronecker descriptors** (Buchholz; Donatelli; Plateau's stochastic automata networks) represent a composed system's operator as a sum of tensor products of per-block operators plus a *synchronization correction* — and the entire engineering difficulty of that school is exactly the correction term, the part that does not factor. The structural-invariant version of the same fact (per-block semiflows recombined with an interface-coupling correction) is studied in modular invariant analysis. The honest positioning is therefore:

- **Not new:** the recombination operator (direct-sum-plus-interface-correction), the observation that the interface correction is where the difficulty lives, and the qualitative notion that a property "factors" over a decomposition. Φ is a *qualitative shadow* of the Kronecker-descriptor correction — it does not compute the correction, it *measures the residual the correction would have to account for*.
- **What is new, claimed only this much:** (i) the **residual framing** — turning the compositional school's correction term into a *signed, per-property, theorem-zeroed, minimized-over-cuts measurement* with a checkable witness, rather than a verification step; (ii) the **Φ_inv–Rank-Theorem link** (§4.2), *pending the literature check*; and (iii) the **corpus measurement** — running these residuals over the MCC NUPN corpus indexed by net class, which to my knowledge no one has reported.

Φ asks the compositional school's question backward. They ask: *given the parts and the correction, reconstruct the whole.* Φ asks: *over all cuts, which decomposition makes the correction smallest — and how small can it get?* The minimizing cut is the answer, and the residual at that cut is the measurement. That is the one genuinely different question, and it is small enough to state without inflation.

## 6. The IIT fence — kept verbatim in spirit

The schema's shape — a *minimum over partitions of a whole-versus-product distance, with the minimizing partition as witness* — is borrowed from the mathematical kernel of Integrated Information Theory, and from nothing else in IIT. The fence from [`principles.md`](principles.md) and [`latent-architecture.md`](latent-architecture.md) is kept here without softening:

> petrivet borrows only the **shape** of IIT's Φ: the minimum, over a partition lattice, of a distance between a whole and the product of its parts, with the minimum information partition as the witnessing cut. It imports **none** of IIT's substance. It does **not** import the cause-effect repertoire, the intrinsic perspective, the exclusion postulate, or the stochastic semantics that IIT's Φ requires — and could not, because the incidence matrix $C$ is **integer** and the analysis is **qualitative**: there is no probability metric over which to take an intrinsic-difference, no distribution to partition, no stochastic dynamics. The factorization residuals of this essay are a statement about **nets, not minds**. The step from a factorization residual to consciousness is metaphor, it is not taken here, and it lies outside this work. **IIT appears nowhere in the repository** — no Φ, no irreducibility postulate, no partition in IIT's sense — and these residuals do not introduce it; they introduce a measurement of how much a Petri net's *boundedness* or *conservation laws* fail to factor over its declared decomposition, which is a fact about the net and the cut.

The fence is load-bearing precisely because the shape is suggestive. Suggestiveness is not a claim. The residuals are integer or token-valued, basis-free, and qualitative; importing IIT's apparatus would mean *inventing* stochastic structure the analyzer does not have, which is the same overreach §1 dissolved, in a different costume.

## 7. Verdict, and the connection to the planner

Dissolve the scalar. Keep the schema. Build the two instances. Let the claim be the measurement, not the metaphysics.

- **Dissolved:** the net-level scalar Φ_PN (incommensurable units), the boolean Φ (assume-guarantee, already mature), and the "needs all four roots / built to compute it" necessity (aesthetic, not mathematical, and disproved by the ease of writing two more capstones).
- **Kept:** the schema — minimize over a decomposition lattice a *per-property* residual with a genuine composition rule, well-signed, theorem-zeroed, non-vacuous, witness-bearing.
- **Built (BACKLOG H2a, H2b):** **Φ_bound** = $\min_\pi \sum_p (b^{\otimes}(p) - b(N)_p)$ over the NUPN/S-component cuts — monotone, zero on live bounded free-choice and strongly-connected T-nets, infinite on cross-block-synchronized boundedness; and **Φ_inv** = $\min_\pi (\dim\ker(C^\top) - \dim\bigoplus_B \mathrm{Inv}^\circ(B))$ — integer, basis-free, non-negative under the explicit interface correction, with the conjectured Rank-Theorem link as its novel and *falsifiable* edge.
- **Reported:** the two distributions over the MCC NUPN corpus, indexed by `NetClass` — the measurement of how modular real Petri nets are, which is the deliverable and which no one has published.

The connection to the rest of the project is concrete, and it is why these residuals earn their place rather than merely surviving. In the certified-reduction planner ([`the-sequel.md`](the-sequel.md); BACKLOG D8/F), the central action is **split** — decompose a residual net into independently analyzable parts, prove each, and `lift` the part-certificates back to the whole. A split is a *good move exactly when the property factors over the cut*, which is to say exactly when the local Φ is small. **Φ is the applicability oracle for the split action**: a cut with $\Phi_{\mathrm{bound}} = 0$ (or $\Phi_{\mathrm{inv}} = 0$) is one where part-wise analysis recombines to the whole for free, the best possible move; a cut with large Φ is a move that will not pay off, because the recombination must carry a correction the planner cannot yet certify. The residual that began as a metaphysical capstone ends as something humbler and more useful: a cheap, structural, theorem-backed *hint to the planner about where the net wants to be cut*. That is the honest shape of the idea — not the quantity the architecture was built to compute, but a measurement the architecture happens to make possible, and a heuristic the planner can consult. The mirage is gone; the instrument remains.
